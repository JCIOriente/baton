Palette: ink `#111418`, ground `#F5F6F4`, signal orange `#FF5A1F`.
The wordmark uses Space Grotesk Bold, converted to outlines.

The mark shows two batons meeting in a handoff zone: human and agent, one holder at a time,
handed off explicitly.

- Use the lockup that matches the background.
- Keep clear space on all sides of at least one quarter of the square mark's width.
- Keep the batons' original colors. Do not recolor them.
- Scale proportionally. Do not stretch.
